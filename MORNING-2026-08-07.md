## TASK 4 — What I already verified so you don't have to

**Member picks are NOT at risk from this repair. Exposure measured at ZERO.**

I flagged a real hazard yesterday and then measured it against production; it does
not bite here. Both mechanisms are real, and both are documented in
`PLAN-NFL-IMPORT-SCOPE.md` §6b for the next time:

- confidence values are validated **only at submit** and scoring just sums the
  stored numbers, so changing the number of games in a week can leave a value out
  of range or colliding;
- Survivor/Margin picks are keyed by **week** holding a **team**, so a pick does
  not follow its game across weeks — a stranded Survivor pick grades as survived
  with no strike, and a stranded Margin pick is skipped entirely with no `-14`.

Measured before the repair:

```
pools scanned: 134   NFL preseason pools: 7
(a) NFL_PICKEM pools with confidenceMode === true ......... 3
(b) confidence entries holding week 1 or 2 picks ........... 4
    of those, stored value out of range after the move .... 0
    of those, colliding value inside week 2 after ......... 0
(c) Survivor/Margin pools with a stranded week 1 pick ...... 0
```

Why the zeros are real rather than a filter stepping over gaps:

- all four confidence entries hold exactly one week-1 pick, on `espn_401873271`, at
  confidence **16**, with **0 MISSING** values — and 16 is the only legal value in
  a one-game week, so every sheet is legal before and after;
- week 2 only **widens** (`[7..16]` → `[1..16]`), so nothing arriving there can go
  out of range;
- the surviving week-1 game is **CAR@ARI**, and the Margin entry that looked exposed
  had picked **ARI**, which still plays in week 1. Not stranded;
- nothing has been scored yet on any affected pool (`scoredWeeks`, `publishedWeeks`,
  `scoredThroughWeek` all unset; `weeklyScores` empty; `seasonTotal` 0), so there is
  no banked per-week value to go stale.

> ⚠️ **My first census reported one exposed Margin entry and it was a FALSE
> POSITIVE** — it asked "is this team in a moving game", which flags ARI, when the
> right question is "in a moving game **and** left with no game in the week
> afterwards". Fixed, and the trap is written into the script and the plan, because
> the next re-file will meet it again.

**One thing the repair silently FIXES.** Week 1 currently contains both
`espn_401873271` CAR@**ARI** and `espn_401873640` **ARI**@LV, and `scoreMarginWeek`
resolves a pick by first-match array order. So an ARI Margin pick is, today, graded
against whichever game the query returned first. One entry is in that state. After
the repair the ambiguity is gone — if that grade changes, it is the fix, not a
regression.
