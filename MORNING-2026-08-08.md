### Rollback — one config write, at any point

Set **`system/config.nflAutoScore.enabled`** to `false`. The next run reads it,
logs `disabled`, and does nothing. Setting `dryRun` back to `true` is the softer
half-step: the job keeps reporting but stops writing.

### Two things to know before you watch the counters

1. **`ok: true` is not "everything got scored".** Only `poolsFailed > 0` makes a
   run unhealthy. `overflow`, `poolsSkipped` and `survivorQueuedDeferred` are
   reported and none of them flips the colour. Read the counters.
2. **`survivorQueuedDeferred` above 0 needs you, and nothing will page you.** A
   late ESPN correction to an already-scored Survivor week is deliberately
   **refused** rather than replayed, because replaying it corrupts the
   elimination ordering. It will not self-heal. If you see that counter move,
   tell me and we repair by hand.

**#396 — five-minute scoring, and a guard against a silent monitor lie.** The
scorer moves `'*/10'` → `'*/5'`, because staleness is the **sum** of ESPN
ingestion and grading and the old worst case was 15 minutes. `timeoutSeconds`
moves 540 → 270 with it, since the 540 existed only to keep two runs from
overlapping inside a 10-minute gap. And `SCHEDULED_JOB_EXPECTATIONS` — the
registry that decides when a job is reported dead — is now checked against the
actual cron strings by a test, because nothing kept them in step and a registry
that is slower than reality lets a dead job look fresh.

**#397 — the import button, and why "no logs" proved nothing.** `validated()`
logs nothing at all unless the payload carries a correlation id — not on entry,
not on success, and not when it rejects the call at the auth or role gate. So for
`importNFLSchedule`, "zero invocation logs" was equally consistent with success,
with a permission denial, and with the click never arriving. It now carries one,
as does `scoreNFLWeek` — the manual scoring fallback, and the worst possible
place to have no trace. The button also no longer shows a pointer cursor while
disabled, and its result banner moved from above the form to below the button.
**The original root cause is still open**, and
[TICKET-IMPORT-BUTTON-NOOP.md](TICKET-IMPORT-BUTTON-NOOP.md) §3 records the one
