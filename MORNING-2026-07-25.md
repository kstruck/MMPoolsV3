You ruled (2026-07-25): **Survivor AND Margin pools use a WEEKLY hard deadline
before the first game**, manager-configurable at **60 / 30 / 5 min** before
kickoff; once locked, no picks. That's the right call — a hard deadline makes their
picks immutable after lock, which makes **all three pool types live-scorable
per-game** (better than the A/B options I'd posed; no per-game-freeze needed).

(`submitNFLPicks` throws `WEEK_LOCKED`), the deadline is first-kickoff−buffer, and
the buffer is manager-editable. Gaps → **PR-0** (now first in the build):

- Survivor/Margin default to `PER_GAME` today → **server-force `WEEKLY`** (a plain
  manager save must not be able to revert it).
