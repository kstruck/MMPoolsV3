## 2. ⚠️ ONE DECISION I NEED FROM YOU (shapes G1's scope)

**Live scoring for Survivor & Margin pools.** Codex surfaced a real constraint:
a Survivor/Margin **weekly pick stays swappable across games** until the week's
last game locks (a member can replace a locked Thursday pick with a Sunday team —
`submitNFLPicks` only checks the *new* team's lock). So a Thursday result can't be
safely published for those pools while the pick can still change.

- **Option A (what the plan builds):** live per-game scoring for **Pick'em**;
  **Survivor/Margin update at week-complete**. Pick'em is the pilot's primary mode
  (the wizard hardcodes STRAIGHT Pick'em), so most pools get true game-by-game
  now. **My recommendation** — zero product risk, ships fastest.
- **Option B:** also make S/M live by adding a submit-side guard that **freezes a
  weekly pick once its selected game locks**. Closes the hole, enables game-by-game
  for S/M — but changes pick UX and is its own product + PR.

**Tell me A or B.** A unblocks the G1 build immediately; B adds a submit-side PR
first. Detail: PLAN §3b (the boxed "KEVIN DECISION").
