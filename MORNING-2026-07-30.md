### What it fixed

You found the Buy-In Ledger reporting `$0` projected, `$0` collected, `0%`
clearing rate and *"no members matching filter criteria"* — beside a Member
Roster panel, **on the same page**, listing those members correctly.

**Root cause.** Every figure on the card and in the Advanced Payment Ledger modal
was `entries`-derived. No write can fix that: `setPaidStatus` mirrors display
fields onto the entry only `if (entrySnap.exists)`, so an entry-backed **reader**
cannot see a member who never submitted one. That is the half of D13 that P1
could not reach.

Two more money figures were wrong without needing an empty roster:

- the fee defaulted to `entryFee || 20`, so a **free pool projected $20 a head**
  nobody owed;
- Clearing Rate divided by entry holders, so a pool where one of four members had
  paid — and only that member had an entry — read **100%**.

### How it was fixed

There were already **three** derivations of this data: the roster merge in
`NFLManagerView`, the dues maths in `PaymentsPanel` (hardened over four codex
rounds under PLAN-PAYMENT-TRUTH P3), and this card's entries-only version. The
disagreement **was** the multiplicity, so the first two moved into
`src/utils/poolRoster.ts` unchanged — comments included — and all three surfaces
now read from there. Same one-definition fix as #315, #319 and HANDOFF item 11.
