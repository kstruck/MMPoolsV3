Your ask (row I2): *"In the pool setup wizard, give options for tie-breakers,

So today, a weekly pool works like this: the app scores the week, you read the
numbers, and **you** decide the winner and how a tie breaks. That is not broken

options for tie-breakers" sits on top of a feature that does not exist yet.

Which gives you two very different tickets, and **§2 of the plan is the
sign-off gate**:

- **Option A — settings only.** A wizard + manager choice of
  `MNF combined (both games)` / `MNF last game` / `none`, which changes what the
  sheet asks for, what the recap's closest-guess line measures, and what the
  house rules say. You still call the winner. Small, shippable before kickoff.
- **Option B — A plus a real weekly winner**, computed and published by the
  scorer. Needs a per-week ranking that does not exist, a decision about what
  happens when the tiebreaker *itself* ties, and a publication rule that
  survives mid-Sunday. Lands in the live scorer, and when it is wrong it is
  wrong about money.

**My recommendation: A now, B after launch.** Taking the weekly ruling off the
commissioners three weeks before kickoff, in a scorer that has never run a live
season, is a bigger change than the sentence sounds.

## §4 — 📋 THE AUDIT YOU ASKED FOR: live in-day scoring and Correct/Incorrect

Your words:

**Short answer: yes for Pick'em, and it is better than you may realise — a
member's own sheet colours each game green or red the moment that game ends,
with no scorer involved at all. But it is genuinely missing on Survivor and
Margin, and no surface anywhere shows a per-pick ✓/✗ for OTHER players.**

Everything below is measured against `origin/main` @ `0572babc`.

### 4a. The machinery — when does a pick actually become scored?

Two jobs, both every 5 minutes, both live:

| Job | Cadence | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `syncNFLScoresJob` | `*/5` ([nflSchedule.ts:603](functions/src/nflSchedule.ts:603)) | Pulls ESPN, writes live scores + status into `nfl_games`. A game reaches `FINAL` here |
| `nflAutoScoreJob` | `*/5` ([nflAutoScore.ts:659](functions/src/nflAutoScore.ts:659)) | Grades every live pool. `system/config.nflAutoScore {enabled:true, dryRun:false}` |

**It grades per game as each game ends — not Tuesday.** This is the crux of your
question and the answer is the good one. Every mid-week pass runs *provisional*
(`provisional: !isWeekComplete(...)`, [nflAutoScore.ts:319](functions/src/nflAutoScore.ts:319)),
and a provisional pass grades exactly the games that are **both** concluded
**and** past their own lock:

```ts
// functions/src/nflPools.ts:1132-1137
const revealed = (g) => isTerminalGame(g) && gameLockClosed(g);
const gradableGames = provisional ? games.filter(revealed) : games;
```

So the 1pm games are graded by ~4:20pm, and the pool's standings totals move
with them. **Worst case latency from a game ending to a member's total moving:
about 10 minutes** — up to 5 for ESPN's FINAL to land, up to 5 more for the
scorer's next pass.

Two honest caveats:

- A `FINAL` the feed reports **no scores** for does not count as concluded
  ([weekCompletion.ts](functions/src/lib/weekCompletion.ts) `isTerminalGame`).
  The scorer waits and self-heals on the next pass. Correct, but it means a
  broken payload shows as "not scored yet" rather than as an error.
- **Survivor and Margin are different by design.** Their per-week result is held
  until the **weekly lock has passed** (`weeklyPickReady`,
  [nflPools.ts:1149-1181](functions/src/nflPools.ts:1149)). Publishing a
  survival mid-Sunday would reveal a member's pick while they could still change
  it, and a Margin 0 would flip when the game ends. So Survivor/Margin members
  see nothing move until the weekly deadline has passed.

**The thing worth understanding about the Pick'em sheet:** its green/red does
**not** come from the scorer. It is computed in the browser by `gradePick`
([src/utils/pickemResult.ts:39](src/utils/pickemResult.ts:39)) straight off the
live `nfl_games` document, using the same rule the server uses — including the
ATS branch, and pinned against the real server function by
`tests/pickem-result-parity.test.ts`. So it colours the instant `syncNFLScoresJob`
writes FINAL, without waiting for a scoring pass. That is why the answer to "can
members watch their day go" is yes for Pick'em.
