## §6 — What else moved overnight

**The deploy-order lesson is now in the skill, not just in HANDOFF.**
`mmp-deploy-and-operate` §1a carries it. That was your suggestion and it was
meant to be small — codex then found that the version I first wrote was
**wrong**, and the correction is worth knowing:

I had written that rebuilding early is always free, because "a revocation only
ever narrows". That is true of **#414** and not in general. A rules change that
**both** revokes a read the old client makes **and** grants one the new client
needs has **no safe order**: rules first kills the old client, rebuild first
kills the new one. The skill now carries a 2×2 table for classifying the diff,
and for the mixed case a **compatibility-ruleset split** — deploy a union
ruleset, rebuild, then tighten — which has to be planned before the branch is
cut rather than discovered on deploy night.

#414 itself was a pure revocation (its replacement was a *callable*, which rules
do not gate), which is exactly why the simpler rule looked universal.

## §7 — Not built, and why

**Bracket / playoff / squares pick sheets — deliberately out of scope.** The
shared row components assume a two-sided NFL matchup with a kickoff, a TV listing
and a betting line. A bracket has none of those and a squares grid is not a row.
Forcing them in would be worse than leaving them alone. Said on the PR too.

**"Pick from the Pool Home tab" — read as the entry point, not a second sheet,
and no code was written for it.** Pool Home already promotes a
**Make My Picks** / **Edit My Picks** button (and the whole focus-game panel is a
click target); both switch to the picks tab in one press and both already work
for pick'em. Rendering a second live copy of a stateful sheet — drafts, sticky
bar, confidence selects — on another tab is real bug surface, and making Pool
Home *be* the pick sheet is a product decision rather than a wiring one.
**If you meant the sheet rendered inline on Pool Home, say so and it is a small
follow-up.** The My Entry / Picks tab is untouched and still works.

**T-C (auto-pick on a missed deadline) and T-D (margin missed-pick penalty) — not
started.** Both are plan-gated and neither plan exists. The night went to #417 and
to the money-bug plan instead, on the reasoning that a live mis-billing beats an
unbuilt feature.

⚠️ **One thing to know before T-D's plan gets written:** its spec assumes a
missed *Survivor* pick already scores as a loss, and **nobody has checked whether
the engine does that.** The plan's first step is to measure it, not to change it.
