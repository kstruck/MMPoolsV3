## 1. What the drill found

`functions/src/__tests__/emulator/hofChaosDrill.emulator.test.ts` — the feed
going wrong on a one-game preseason week. The HOF week is the only week where a
single game is both the whole week and the whole season, so every "the other
games will cover for it" assumption in the scorer is unavailable.

**The clean results first, because they are real and you should know them:**

- **Corrections converge completely.** A restated final score drained through
  `nfl_rescore_queue` flips the entries, the member-readable standings **and the
  `seasonHistory` champion**. Finalization really is the re-runnable overwrite it
  is documented to be.
- **Postponement waits correctly**, and a game left SCHEDULED >12h past its own
  kickoff is reported STALLED rather than pending in silence.
- **A `FINAL → IN_PROGRESS` regression is a complete no-op**, because
  `finalizedAt` drops the pool from candidate selection — with the queue tier
  still available if the regression turns out to be real.

**The five defects:**

| ID | Severity | What |
|---|---|---|
| NFL7-4 | **Worst** | A `FINAL` the feed reported **no scores** for grades as a real 0-0. For Survivor that is a TIE, and a tie is a strike — so at `maxStrikes: 0`, **a field ESPN dropped eliminates the member who picked correctly.** |
| NFL7-3 | High | Same root cause, Pick'em: a PUSH for everyone. The week then reads complete, the recap is written, and the one-game season **finalizes** on a result nobody played. |
| NFL7-1 | High | A Survivor non-submitter is struck for a week whose **only game was cancelled** — there was no legal pick to make. On the HOF week that ends every non-submitter's pilot on night one. |
| NFL7-2 | High | The same case in Margin, charged −14. Separate code path, so it needed its own fix. |
| NFL7-5 | Latent | `isWeekComplete([])` was vacuously true. Not reachable today; the failure would have been silent and total. |

The root cause of NFL7-3/4 is worth understanding, because the codebase was
*half* right: the importer goes out of its way to omit `scores` on a partial
payload precisely so a dropped field stays distinguishable from a genuine zero.
Nothing downstream repeated the distinction — every engine read
`game.scores?.home ?? 0`, which collapses the two cases back together.

**codex raised this twice (rounds 5 and 6) and I did not fix it, because the fix
is a production-data write and that is your call.**

A `FINAL` document written *before* this deploy has no `scoresMissing` marker.

**I believe the exposure is nil, and I could not prove it.** The harmful case

in a pool **not yet finalized**. Under the old code that game graded as a real

### Step-by-step — the check (about 60 seconds)

1. Open <https://console.firebase.google.com/project/gridiron-gamble-uzuqo/firestore/data>
   in your browser.
2. In the left-hand collection list, click **`nfl_games`**.
3. Click the **filter icon** (funnel, above the document list) → **Add filter**.
4. Set the field to `status`, the operator to `==`, and the value to `FINAL`.
   Click **Apply**.
5. **What you should see:** either *"No documents match"*, or a list of finished
   games. Click through a few of the listed documents and look for a **`scores`**
   map in each one's field list.
6. **The answer you are looking for:** does ANY document in that filtered list
   have **no `scores` field at all**?
   - **No document lacks it (or the list is empty)** → the finding is moot. Merge
