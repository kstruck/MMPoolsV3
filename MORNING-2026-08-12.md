### 1c. Deploy functions — FIRST

⚠️ **THE ORDER IS LOAD-BEARING, AND IT IS THREE STEPS, NOT TWO: functions →
Coolify → rules.**

#414's rules edit removes the commissioner's raw entry read. Two different things
have to be in place before that read can safely go away:

1. **the callable that replaces it** (`getPoolPicks`), which ships with the
   **functions**; and
2. **a client that calls it**, which ships with the **Coolify rebuild**.

⚠️ **The second one is the trap, and it is the one a "functions then rules" order
gets wrong.** The frontend live in production right now is the pre-#414 build,
and it still subscribes to the raw entries collection for managers. Deploying the
rules before the rebuild revokes that read out from under a client that is still
making it — **every commissioner's standings tab goes blank or errors for the
whole length of the rebuild.** Deploying `getPoolPicks` does not help, because
the live client does not know to call it. (Found by codex reviewing this runbook.)

Going the other way is safe: between the rebuild and the rules deploy, the NEW
client is live under the OLD rules. It never calls the raw read at all — that
subscription is deleted in #414 — so nothing breaks; commissioners simply retain
a permission the client no longer uses, for a few minutes.

⚠️ **This deploys into a LIVE scorer.** `nflAutoScoreJob` runs every 5 minutes.
Nothing in either PR is in the scoring path, but the deploy touches the whole
fleet.
