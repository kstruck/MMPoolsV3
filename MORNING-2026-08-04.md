because a Pool Credit or a 100%-off coupon also leaves `pricePaid` at 0 while
the commissioner genuinely settled.

⚠️ **YOUR CALL:** a genuine free-allocation pool therefore gets **no** banner. If
you want one there it needs different words — *"hosting is free for this pool"*,
not *"fees paid"*. Say so and it is a one-line addition.
