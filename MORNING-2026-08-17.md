## 2. What T2 does (30-second version)

- `submitNFLPicks` accepts `entryIndex` (1..max, default 1) and `entryName`.
- Entry #1 is still `entries/{uid}` (byte-identical for every existing pool);
  entry n ≥ 2 is `entries/e{n}:{uid}`. Every entry doc now carries
  `ownerUid` + `entryIndex`.
- The cap is enforced from how many entry docs the owner already has, inside
  the transaction (two concurrent first-submits cannot exceed it).
- Member Record: `playableEntryCount` + `entries` map (id → index + name, no
  picks). `feeOwed = entryFee × liable entries`. Fee edits cascade × count.
- `setPaidStatus` mirrors PAID/UNPAID onto EVERY entry the member owns and the
  ledger amount is `feeOwed` (the multiplied figure).
- A PAID member who adds an entry flips to UNPAID; a `MARKED_UNPAID` ledger
  line (actor `system`) says why and what they had paid.
- `pool.entryCount` is server-maintained (liable entries): 0 at create,
  derived from Member Records when absent, moved by join/submit/proxy, and
  initialised by `updatePoolSettings` the first time the max is raised.
- `proxyPick` + `executeSurvivorRebuy` take `entryIndex`; the manager view's
  proxy picker now lists ENTRIES; a new "Entries per Player" number field on
  the settings form is the raise control (server refuses lowering).
- `MULTI_ENTRY_WIZARD_ENABLED` **stays `false`** — see §3, this is a
  deliberate deviation from your prompt.
