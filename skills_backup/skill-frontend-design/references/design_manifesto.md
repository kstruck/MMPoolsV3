# The "Anti-Slop" Design Manifesto

## 1. The "No AI Slop" Rules
The following traits are banned because they signal "Lazy AI Generation":
* ❌ **Banned Colors:** "AI Purple" gradients on white backgrounds.
* ❌ **Banned Fonts:** Inter, Roboto, Open Sans (unless specifically requested for utility).
* ❌ **Banned Layouts:** Three centered cards with icons on top.
* ❌ **Banned Copy:** "Unlock your potential," "Elevate your experience."

## 2. Typography Guidelines
* **Display:** Use distinctive fonts (Playfair Display, Syne, Space Grotesk, Chivo).
* **Body:** distinct but readable (DM Sans, Satoshi, Cabinet Grotesk).
* **Pairing:** Always pair a "Character" header with a "Utility" body.

## 3. Motion Principles
* **Stagger:** Never animate everything at once. Use `animation-delay`.
* **Speed:** UI animations should be fast (200-300ms).
* **Trigger:** Use scroll-based reveals (IntersectionObserver) to create depth.
