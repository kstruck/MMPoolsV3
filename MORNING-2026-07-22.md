**The mistake, and how it was caught.** I built PR #255 on a branch cut from a
stale base rather than from `origin/main`. Its diff against `origin/main`
included a 125-line `package-lock.json` reversal — it would have **reverted the
`fast-uri` security fix from #254** while looking like a clean security PR.

Codex's review output showed a merge-base of `abda2f5` instead of `305b36b`,
which is what made me look. I rebuilt the branch from `origin/main`, cherry-
picked the single commit, and re-ran every gate. Verified after: `fast-uri`
3.1.4, and the PR touches exactly four files.

Discipline I am applying from here: **always `git checkout -B <branch>
origin/main` after a fetch**, and always read codex's stated merge-base rather
than assuming `--base origin/main` resolved the way I expected.
