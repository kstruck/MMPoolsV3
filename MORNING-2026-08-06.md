### 2.3 The other messages you might get, and what each means

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **`Week 1 scored successfully.`** | Done. Standings written, recap written, week finalized. | Nothing. Move to the next pool. |
| **`Week 1 scored provisionally — live standings updated.`** | You clicked **before** the game was final. No recap and no finalization. Games that have not concluded are withheld. **⚠️ But this is NOT inert — read the box below before you rely on it.** | Wait for FINAL, then **click the button again**. |
| **`Another scoring pass is already running for this pool; nothing was written.`** | A scoring lease is held — you (or a double-click) started a pass that has not finished. **Literally nothing was written by this attempt.** | Wait ~30 seconds and click again. |
| **`ACTIVE_GAMES: Cannot score the week while 1 games are still active.`** | The game is not final and the clicker is **not** a SUPER_ADMIN. | Wait for FINAL. You will not normally see this — see the warning below. |
| **`No games found to score for Week 1.`** | The week selector is on a week with no games. | Change the week selector to **HOF Weekend**. |
| Anything starting `Scoring failed.` | Genuine error. | Screenshot it, do not retry blindly, ping me. |

> ### ⚠️ You are a SUPER_ADMIN, so an early click does NOT stop you
>
> The `ACTIVE_GAMES` guard **exempts SUPER_ADMIN** (`nflPools.ts`: `if
> (activeGamesCount > 0 && userRole !== 'SUPER_ADMIN')`). So if you press the
> button before the game is final, you will **not** get an error — you will get
> the *provisional* message in row 2 above.
>
> **A provisional pass is NOT inert.** An earlier draft of this doc said it
> applies no strikes and no penalties. That is wrong, and cross-model review
> caught it before you read it. What `weeklyPickReady`
> (`nflPools.ts`) actually does:
>
> | Situation at the moment you click | Written on a provisional pass? |
> |---|---|
> | Before the weekly lock — anyone | **No.** Nothing at all. |
> | After the lock, member **made a pick**, game not final | **No.** Withheld until the game concludes. |
> | After the lock, member made **NO pick** | ✅ **YES — the Survivor strike / Margin −14 IS applied.** |
> | Either way | No recap, no finalization. |
>
> ⏰ **"The weekly lock" is BEFORE kickoff, not at it.** It is
> `first kickoff − lockBufferMinutes`, and for Survivor and Margin that buffer is
> snapped to one of **60 / 30 / 5 minutes**, defaulting to **5**
> (`shared/weeklyHardLock.ts`). So against an 8:00 PM ET kickoff the deadline is
> **7:55, 7:30 or 7:00 PM ET** depending on the pool's setting — never 8:00. A
> click at 7:56 PM is already *after* the lock on a default pool.
>
> ⚠️ **And the pool's Settings buffer is a CEILING on the deadline, not the
> deadline.** Survivor and Margin freeze the week's hard lock into
> `hardLockByWeek` the first time it is enforced, and that freeze may only ever
> move **earlier** (`resolveHardWeekLock`). So a pool whose buffer was 60 and is
> now 5 still locks at the frozen 7:00 PM — widening the buffer cannot reopen a
> week. **Do not compute the exact minute from Settings and trust it.** If it
> matters, just wait for `Completed Games: 1 / 1` to go green, which is after
> every possible deadline.
>
> That third row is deliberate, not a bug: the no-pick penalty is due at the
> **weekly deadline**, not at FINAL, so a member who missed the lock has genuinely
> earned it. It is also idempotent — strikes are recorded against a `strikeWeeks`
> ledger, so scoring again later does not double-strike. But it means a click any
> time after that deadline **does write to member records**, and you should not
> think of it as a free look.
>
> The card also warns you: **"Some games are still active. SuperAdmins may
> override."** appears under the button whenever the week is not fully final.
>
> **✅ SIMPLEST RULE, AND THE ONE TO FOLLOW: wait until `Completed Games: 1 / 1`
> is green, then click. Do not click early to "see what happens".**

> ### ✅ RE-CLICKING IS SAFE
>
> Scoring is **idempotent**. Survivor strikes are recorded against a
> `strikeWeeks` ledger (`248f61a`), so a second pass does not double-strike
> anyone; standings are recomputed, not accumulated. The old "one click only"
> warning was **wrong** and was retracted in #376.
>
> So: if you are unsure whether a click landed, **click again**. That is the
> correct response, not a risk.

### 5.4 T4 / T6 — the wizard (#383)

The season field was free-typed and defaulted to **2025**, on four wizards. Since
`nfl_games` is keyed on `season` as a **string**, a pool stamped with the wrong
season renders **no games at all** — same blast radius as the `seasonType` NaN
bug in #319, reachable by typing. It is now a read-only **2026**, from one
constant, still sent to the server (the schema requires it, and `shared/` was
deliberately not touched).

`CURRENT_SEASON` is a constant and not `new Date().getFullYear()` on purpose: the
2026 postseason is played in **January 2027**, so a clock-derived value would
start stamping new playoff pools `2027` at midnight on New Year's Eve.

**Self-review caught something codex round 1 missed** and it is the reason for
§4 item 2: the four wizards still listed `fields: ['season']` on the step whose
input had just been removed, which would have made the **Next** button depend on
react-hook-form's behaviour for an unregistered field. The reasoning says it is
fine; if the reasoning is wrong, **no pool can be created at all**. Removed the
entry rather than argue it.

Terms of Service is now a link (`/terms`, new tab) with `stopPropagation`, so
clicking through to read the terms does not silently tick the consent box.
